Fundamental Physics of Colloid Electrospray Thruster Degradation

Electrospray propulsion relies on extracting sub-nanometer ionic liquid droplets ($\text{EMI-BF}_4$ or $\text{EMI-TFSI}$) through porous tungsten micro-emitters via intense electric fields ($E \sim 10^9\text{ V/m}$). Over mission durations required for the Laser Interferometer Space Antenna (LISA, $>10\text{ years}$ drag-free control), electrochemical faradaic reactions, double-layer oxidation, and gas bubbling destroy sub-micronewton precision.

1. Taylor-Melcher EHD Balance & Meniscus Stresses

At the tip of every micro-emitter, fluid equilibrium between surface tension $\gamma$, hydraulic back-pressure $\Delta P$, and electrostatic Maxwell stress $\tau_{\text{Maxwell}}$ dictates the formation of the Taylor cone:

$$\tau_n = \frac{1}{2}\varepsilon_0 \left( E_n^2 - E_t^2 \right) = \frac{2\gamma}{R_{\text{apex}}} + \Delta P_{\text{hyd}}$$

The steady-state mass flow rate $\dot{m}$ and generated micro-thrust $T$ under single-cone pure ionic emission mode satisfy:

$$T = \dot{m} v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2 q_i V_{\text{emitter}}}{m_i}} \cdot I_{\text{beam}} = \sum_{k=1}^{N_{\text{tips}}} \sqrt{2 \left(\frac{q}{m}\right)_k V_{\text{ext}}} \cdot I_k$$

Key Metric: LISA requirement stipulates continuous thrust noise below $\delta F \le 0.1 \text{ nN}/\sqrt{\text{Hz}}$ from $0.1\text{ mHz}$ to $1\text{ Hz}$.

2. Electrochemical Boundary-Layer Degradation

When thrusters operate in continuous DC mode, ionic fluid anions accumulate at the porous tungsten interface. Beyond the electrochemical stability window ($\Delta V_{\text{window}} \approx 4.2\text{ V}$), Faradaic current causes irreversible oxidation and gas evolution:

$$\text{W} + 3\text{BF}_4^- + 3\text{H}_2\text{O} \longrightarrow \text{WO}_3(\text{crust}) + 6\text{H}^+ + 3\text{BF}_4^- + 6e^-$$

The growth rate of this passivating oxide crust follows parabolic boundary kinetics:

$$\frac{d\delta_{\text{oxide}}}{dt} = \frac{k_p}{\delta_{\text{oxide}}} \exp\left(-\frac{\Delta G_{\text{act}}}{k_B T}\right) \cdot \left(\frac{J_{\text{faradaic}}}{J_0}\right)$$

Physical Consequence: $\text{WO}_3$ passivation starves capillary feed channels, blunts tips ($R_{\text{apex}} \uparrow$), and generates catastrophic micro-droplet sputtering.

3. Real-Time (Q, Z) Impedance Inversion Observer

Rather than adding heavy external diagnostic mass, the software superimposes a high-frequency sub-threshold AC ripple ($10\text{ kHz} - 1\text{ MHz}$, $V_{\text{AC}} < 5\text{ V}$) onto the high-voltage emitter bus to compute complex double-layer impedance:

$$Z(\omega) = R_{\text{bulk}} + \frac{R_{\text{ct}}}{1 + (j\omega R_{\text{ct}} C_{\text{dl}})^\alpha} + Z_{\text{Warburg}}(\omega)$$

Simultaneously, picoampere current integrators track net emitted charge $Q(t) = \int I_e dt$. The state observer computes the degradation gradient vector $\mathbf{D}$:

$$\mathbf{D}(t) = \nabla_{(Q,Z)} \Phi = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\partial Z}{\partial t} / Z_0 \\ -\frac{\partial Q}{\partial t} / Q_0 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \text{Condition for Polarity Flip: } \|\mathbf{D}\| \ge D_{\text{crit}}$$

Innovation: Detects sub-angstrom surface fouling 120 hours before thrust noise impacts gravitational wave optical benches.
